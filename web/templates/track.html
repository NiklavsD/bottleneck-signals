What "basket since" means

The equal-weight change in the basket's prices from the day of the call to the latest close, before costs. For IN/OUT calls, an OUT call is right if the basket later falls. For tilt calls, compare the basket with its benchmark.

Verify it yourself

Each line's hash is SHA-256 of the entry without its hash field, serialised as compact JSON with sorted keys, and prev must equal the previous line's hash. The script is in the repo: pipeline/monthly.py.
