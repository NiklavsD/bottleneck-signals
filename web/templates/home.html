How it works

From a customs form to a call

01 · Source

Free, dated physical data

Monthly revenue from 1,850+ Taiwan suppliers, Korean customs exports and US import values by product code. Each value is dated to the day it became public.

02 · Measure

A pressure index per bottleneck

Growth compared with the series' own history. It shows whether a bottleneck is tightening and whether that is speeding up or slowing down.

03 · Test

Rules published first

Each rule is hashed and pushed publicly before it is tested on years it never saw. Rules that fail are marked "monitoring" and make no call.

04 · Record

A log nobody can edit

Every monthly call is stored with its prices and chained by SHA-256 on GitHub. Misses stay on the record.
